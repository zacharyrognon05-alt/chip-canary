# Chip Canary: Are Korea's Semiconductor Export Figures Already Priced In?

*Release timing, earnings and market reactions, 2016-2026*

**Abstract.** Korea publishes its chip export figures early, around the 21st and the 1st of each month, while Samsung and SK Hynix, whose business rests largely on chips, report their earnings weeks later. If investors already watch these figures, do the two firms' earnings still tell the market anything new? I dated every figure by the day it became public, rebuilding the first-20-days series myself from more than 120 Korean customs releases, then compared these exports with the earnings reported later and measured how share prices moved on release days, writing down my predictions before seeing any result. Exports known by the 21st track the earnings published two to five weeks later very closely, and for Samsung the link appears to have strengthened during the AI boom. Yet share prices move only moderately more than usual on release days, and the 20-day figure does not move markets more than the monthly one. Whether this information is already priced in on earnings day remains open: the data are consistent with the idea, but too few to establish it.
