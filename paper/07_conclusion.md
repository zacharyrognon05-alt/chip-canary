# 7. Conclusion

This note documents a gap: Korea's chip exports anticipate Samsung's and SK Hynix's earnings closely, several weeks ahead, yet share prices react only moderately when these figures are released. As chips weigh more heavily in Korean exports and in Samsung's results, this signal deserves closer attention. Whether it is already priced in remains open; more quarters and the exact time of each release would settle it. Two extensions seem natural: a comparison with Taiwan, the other major chip exporter, and a study of how US–China export controls shape this signal.
